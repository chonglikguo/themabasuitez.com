Maba Suitez / Merdeka 118

A landmark becoming a daily neighbourhood.

Maba Suitez sits beside a precinct that brings business, hospitality, retail, public space, transport and national heritage into one address.

The tower at the centre

Merdeka 118 rises 678.9 metres above Kuala Lumpur. PNB describes the 118-storey tower as Southeast Asia’s tallest and the world’s second highest, with Grade A offices, an observation experience and Park Hyatt Kuala Lumpur.

Current precinct hospitality material uses the address name Menara Merdeka Maybank. The broader destination remains the Merdeka 118 precinct.

BusinessPNB and Maybank are major occupiers within the tower
HospitalityPark Hyatt Kuala Lumpur operates on the upper levels
ViewThe View at 118 is planned as the precinct’s high-level observation experience
PNB project overviewMerdeka 118 official site

118 Mall puts culture into the retail mix.

The mall’s official site presents an 88 metre sky-lit dome, the Raincloud water feature, a Malaysian Artisan District and Makanizm Food Hall. PNB describes the wider retail component as approximately one million sq ft.

Tenant mix, operating hours, access and event programming are controlled by the mall operator and should be checked directly before planning a visit.

Maba Suitez is close enough for the precinct to feel everyday.
