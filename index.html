Clear answers before the sales conversation.

Maba Suitez is on Jalan Hang Jebat, Kuala Lumpur, beside the Merdeka 118 precinct. The supplied location diagram places MRT Merdeka about 160 metres away.

The latest supplied project summary lists 914 units across two towers. Tower A is shown as 54 storeys, while current plan material shows Tower B rising to Level 50.

Type A is 323 sq ft, Type B is 484 sq ft and Type C or C(M) is 657 sq ft. Final areas, dimensions, uses and specifications must be checked against official developer documents.

The supplied interior concepts indicate hospitality-ready thinking, but operator appointment, furnishing scope, fees, rental terms and participation conditions should be treated as unconfirmed until formal programme documents are issued.
