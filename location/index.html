Maba Suitez / Location

One address. Several versions of Kuala Lumpur.

Walk toward heritage, rail, retail or the city’s newest landmark from Jalan Hang Jebat.

The project site

Maba Suitez is planned on Jalan Hang Jebat, directly opposite the Merdeka 118 precinct. The latest supplied location diagram places Menara Merdeka Maybank about 118 metres away, MRT Merdeka about 160 metres away and Petaling Street about 240 metres away.

These are indicative distances from project material. Actual walking routes, entrances, construction conditions and journey times should be verified at the time of travel.
